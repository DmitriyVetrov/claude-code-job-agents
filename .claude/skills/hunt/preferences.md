## Grades
Any grade fits; a grade below Senior is not a minus when scoring.

## Locations and office days
On-site / hybrid only in the cities above; full remote anywhere the employer can hire
residents of the candidate's country.

## Apply channel
Company careers site first; the job board only when the company has no own form.

## Freshness
Skip postings older than 30 days and postings with 200+ applicants.

## Hard blockers (filter before evaluation)
- Citizenship requirements the candidate does not meet.
- A mandatory language the CV does not list.
- Relocation to a country without a work permit.
